OCEAN OBSERVATIONS

OceanEmbed Satellite continuously observes the ocean's surface to collect key variables. These surface observations provide the foundational data required for our embedding models.

01

SST

The fundamental parameter governing air-sea interactions and shaping marine ecosystems.

02

SSS

A primary indicator of the global water cycle, driven by evaporation and precipitation.

03

SSH

Crucial for understanding ocean circulation and tracking ocean eddies across vast distances.

04

Currents

The conveyor belts of the sea. They redistribute heat and regulate global climate.

05

Winds

The invisible force driving surface ocean circulation and generating waves.

06

SSA

Captures minute variations in the ocean surface indicating sub-mesoscale ocean structures.

These surface observations are processed by our AI-based Satellite Embedding Model, which learns hidden relationships between surface conditions and the ocean depth below.

01

Data harmonization

Aggregates multi-source satellite datasets, regrids to 0.25° × 0.25°, aligns daily time steps, and normalizes missing values.

02

AI Embedding engine

CNNs capture local spatial patterns, Vision Transformers model long-range dependencies, and autoencoders create compact representations.

03

Subsurface reconstruction

A multi-output regression head decodes learned embeddings into depth-wise temperature predictions across every level.
